# Asset attribution

## Flag images

All 195 PNG flags were downloaded from Flagpedia's public flag CDN (`https://flagcdn.com/w320/{code}.png`) on 2026-11-12. Flagpedia states that its flag images are in the public domain, based on vector files from Wikimedia Commons. A backlink is appreciated: https://flagpedia.net/download

## Country codes and names

The ISO alpha-2 codes and common English names in `flags.txt` are a subset of the `mledoze/countries` dataset (https://github.com/mledoze/countries), filtered to the 193 UN member states and supplemented with Vatican City and Palestine. The source dataset is licensed under ODbL 1.0; its full license is included as `ODbL-1.0.txt`.

## Scope

The manifest contains 195 sovereign states: 193 UN member states, Vatican City (Holy See), and Palestine. No dependent territories are included.
